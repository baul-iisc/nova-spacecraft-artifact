gem5-NOVA — IEEE Transactions on Computers artifact tree
========================================================

This checkout is trimmed from gem5-nova-fix1 for the spacecraft benchmark artifact.
It retains the validated RISC-V decode models for:
  - custom trigonometric FP ops (e.g. fsin.d/fcos.d) with TimingSimpleCPU latency stalls
  - 3×3 output-stationary systolic GEMM via sys.sys_mmacd (see src/arch/riscv/isa/decoder.isa)

Build (RISCV, optimised):
  scons build/RISCV/gem5.opt -j$(nproc)

Single-core SE run (matches README-style parameters):
  export GEM5_IEEE_TC_ARTIFACT=1
  ./build/RISCV/gem5.opt --outdir=m5out-hello \\
    configs/learning_gem5/part1/riscv_modified_configs.py \\
    /path/to/spacecraft_xxx*.riscv \\
    --l1i_size=8kB --l1d_size=16kB --l2_size=64kB --system_mem_range=64GB \\
    --clock_freq=500MHz --branch_pred=BiModeBP --prefetcher=None \\
    --cpu_type=TimingSimpleCPU \\
    --l1i_assoc=2 --l1d_assoc=2 \\
    --l1i_tag_latency=2 --l1d_tag_latency=4 \\
    --l1i_data_latency=2 --l1d_data_latency=4 \\
    --l1i_response_latency=2 --l1d_response_latency=4 \\
    --l1i_mshrs=2 --l1d_mshrs=2 \\
    --l1i_tgts_per_mshr=8 --l1d_tgts_per_mshr=8

Optional timing traces (much slower): add --simple_trace

Verification (hardware custom instructions actually run)
---------------------------------------------------------
AtomicSimpleCPU: fast ISA/memory-functional check — does NOT model accelerator
latency or cache contention; useful only as a sanity pass.

TimingSimpleCPU: what the paper simulations use — L1/L2/memory timing PLUS
blocked stalls on trig/matrix shared functional units — required to trust
hardware-variant runs.

Scripts/verify_spacecraft_cpus.zsh runs workload 28_AI (SW/HW/TRIG/SYSTOLIC)
with both CPUs for side-by-side comparison; use ONLY_TIMING=1 when you skip
Atomic. Override WORKLOAD_ROOT to point at another short workload directory.
